Where to stay
near the Cours Ju?

You don’t need to sleep at the Cours Ju to enjoy it. The neighbourhood has few hotels, but it is a few minutes away on foot or by metro from Noailles, the Old Port, Le Panier and Saint-Charles station, where there’s plenty of choice. Here are six addresses, chosen for their easy access to the neighbourhood.

In short

  • The closest: the Mama Shelter, rue de la Loubière, five minutes’ walk from the Cours Julien.
  • Charming hotels: the Hôtel Edmond Rostand, in the 6th, and Le Ryad, near the Canebière.
  • On a budget: the Vertigo, The People and Meininger hostels.
  • Good to know: stay near a station on metro line 2, which stops at the foot of the neighbourhood.
01Five minutes away

In the
neighbourhood

Only one large hotel is really in the neighbourhood, between La Plaine and place Notre-Dame-du-Mont. Walk down in the evening, walk back up at night.

Hotel · Notre-Dame-du-Mont, 6th

Mama Shelter Marseille

The closest to the Cours Julien, five minutes’ walk away. Designer rooms, a restaurant, a bar and a rooftop.

64 rue de la Loubière, 13006 Marseille

Official website ↗

02A quarter of an hour away

Two charming
hotels

Two small independent hotels, far from the chains, each a quarter of an hour’s walk from the Cours Julien.

Hotel · Antiques quarter, 6th

Hôtel Edmond Rostand

A charming three-star hotel between rue de Rome and the Préfecture, with its own café on the ground floor. The Cours Julien is a quarter of an hour away on foot.

31 rue Dragon, 13006 Marseille · Double rooms from €80 to €300 depending on the season

Official website ↗

Hotel · Canebière, 1st

Le Ryad

A small boutique hotel inspired by Moroccan houses, with an inner garden where breakfast is served. Ten minutes’ walk from the Cours Julien, through Noailles.

16 rue Sénac-de-Meilhan, 13001 Marseille · Double rooms from €85 to €225 depending on the season

Official website ↗

03On a budget

Youth
hostels

Dorms, shared kitchens, a bar and new friends: three recent hostels, at the Old Port, in Le Panier and at La Joliette. All are a metro ride or a short walk from the neighbourhood.

Hostel · Old Port, 7th

Vertigo Hostel Marseille

Two hundred metres from the Old Port: air-conditioned dorms with their own bathroom, a shared kitchen and a bar in a vaulted cellar. About twenty minutes’ walk from the Cours Julien.

38 rue Fort-Notre-Dame, 13007 Marseille

Official website ↗

Hostel · Le Panier, 2nd

The People Marseille

A large hostel mixing tradition and street art, a few minutes from Le Panier, the Old Port and the station. Dorms and private rooms, a bar and a restaurant.

12 rue Jean-Trinquet, 13002 Marseille · Dorm beds from €22 a night

Official website ↗

Hotel-hostel · La Joliette, 2nd

Meininger Marseille Centre La Joliette

Half hotel, half hostel: dorms and private rooms, a big lounge, a shared kitchen. Joliette station is on line 2: the Cours Julien is a direct ride, a few stops away.

25-27 rue de Forbin, 13002 Marseille

Official website ↗

04Our tips

Which area
to choose?

On foot

Notre-Dame-du-Mont, Castellane

To walk home after a gig. The 6th arrondissement is central, and quieter than the Old Port at night.

Central

Noailles, the Canebière

At the foot of the neighbourhood, ten minutes’ walk away. Lively by day, noisier at night.

Postcard Marseille

The Old Port, Le Panier

For the sea and the narrow streets. Allow twenty minutes on foot, or take the metro.

Line 2

Saint-Charles, La Joliette

Handy if you arrive by train or ferry: metro line 2 runs directly to Notre-Dame-du-Mont – Cours Julien station.

Frequently asked questions
Where to stay near Cours Julien?

The Mama Shelter, rue de la Loubière, is five minutes’ walk away. The Hôtel Edmond Rostand (6th) and Le Ryad (1st) are a quarter of an hour away. The Vertigo, The People and Meininger hostels are at the Old Port, in Le Panier and at La Joliette.

Is there a youth hostel near the Cours Julien?

Not in the neighbourhood itself. The closest are Vertigo, near the Old Port, and The People, near Le Panier. Meininger, at La Joliette, is on metro line 2, which runs directly to the Cours Julien.

Which area should I stay in to go out at the Cours Julien?

The 6th arrondissement (Notre-Dame-du-Mont, Castellane) to do everything on foot, Noailles and the Canebière to be central, or anywhere near a station on metro line 2.
