The Cours Julien – La Plaine neighbourhood

Between Cours Julien, La Plaine (place Jean-Jaurès) and Notre-Dame-du-Mont, Marseille’s 6th arrondissement is home to a large share of the city’s cultural life and nightlife. Art galleries, street art, cocktail bars, music venues, independent bookshops, restaurants, a community cinema: the neighbourhood mixes genres and crowds.

In 2024, Time Out magazine named it the coolest neighbourhood in the world.

This site lists the places of the neighbourhood. The aim: quickly find somewhere to go tonight, discover a new spot, or simply wander around and explore.
