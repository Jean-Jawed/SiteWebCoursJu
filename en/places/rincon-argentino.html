Rincón Argentino

Argentinian empanada bar

Esteban and Alejandra, from Buenos Aires, hand-make porteño empanadas: beef, chicken, ham and cheese, spinach, humita, fugazzeta… Take away or stay for a glass of Malbec under the Maradona posters. A small Argentinian grocery corner and peña nights.
