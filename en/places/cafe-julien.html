Café Julien

The Espace Julien's small stage, reopened in October 2026

The Café Julien is the Espace Julien's small hall and bar, a training-ground stage that has seen much of the Marseille scene pass through. After seven months of works it reopened on 2 October 2026: by expanding into the building's lobby it has grown from 180 to 280 capacity, with a glass front opening onto the cours Julien, new acoustics and ventilation, and a wheelchair-accessible bar. At night it is a club and a stage for emerging artists: gigs, DJ sets, showcases and release parties, the link between the neighbourhood's small venues and the big hall next door. On Thursdays, 10am to 5pm, it opens by day with free entry: coffee, exhibitions, workshops, talks and local radio shows broadcast live.
