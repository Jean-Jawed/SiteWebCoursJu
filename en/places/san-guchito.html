San Guchito

Chilean sandwich shop on rue Mazagran

A Chilean sandwich shop, local sourcing and everything home-made: mechada con queso (pulled braised beef and melted cheese), milanesa, completos, empanadas, and a vegetarian sandwich that changes with the seasons. On Wednesdays, a traditional Chilean dish, and Latin American music nights from time to time.
