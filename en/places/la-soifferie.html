La Soifferie

Retro cocktail bar on place Paul-Cézanne

Opened in summer 2021 on place Paul-Cézanne, a cocktail bar decorated like a grandmother's living room: retro wallpaper and second-hand furniture. The signature cocktails are named after the team's grandmothers, the menu changes with the seasons and the big terrace fills up as soon as the sun comes out. DJ sets and "Turbo Soiff" nights on some evenings.
