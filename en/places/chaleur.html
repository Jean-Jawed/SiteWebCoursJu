Chaleur

Friendly, community-minded neighbourhood restaurant

On boulevard Chave, a neighbourhood restaurant that lives up to its name ("warmth"): fresh food at small prices, a pavement terrace, pay-it-forward meals, and a programme of DJ sets and community nights. An open, welcoming place for everyone.
