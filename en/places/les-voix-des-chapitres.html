Les Voix des Chapitres

Bookshop for fantasy, sci-fi, manga and young readers

You can't miss it: on the front of 31 cours Julien, Luffy and Totoro stand guard around the sign. Les Voix des Chapitres is an independent bookshop devoted to speculative fiction in all its forms. Fantasy, science fiction and horror novels, comics and manga are shelved by genre, with a big children's corner on a colourful chequered floor. The booksellers know their shelves and are happy to recommend. The month's programme comes out at the start of each month: authors visit almost every week for talks and signings, and the shop even has its own podcast. Open Tuesday to Saturday.
