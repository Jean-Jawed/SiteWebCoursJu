Au comptoir du livre

Café and bookshop, second-hand books and home-made cakes

A café and bookshop with book-lined walls, velvet armchairs, chandeliers and a record collection. People come to read a novel over a hot chocolate or a coffee, with home-made snacks, sweet or savoury (the carrot cake goes fast). Cheap books, breakfast, cocktails and evening events. A few tables outside under the gilded sign.
