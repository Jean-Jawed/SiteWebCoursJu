Garage Comedy Club

Comedy club and tapas bar: stand-up six nights a week

On rue des Trois Frères Barthélémy, just off the cours Julien, the Garage Comedy Club opened in September 2021, set up by a collective of some fifty Marseille comedians determined to make the city France's second stand-up capital. It worked: it has become one of the busiest comedy clubs outside Paris. The place lives up to its name: road signs, a sofa cut from a car body, garage-green walls covered in frames, a pool table and darts in the lounge. At the back is the show room, with a brick wall and a metal shutter behind the mic and just under a hundred seats right up against the stage. Tuesday to Sunday, line-ups of three to seven comedians, from beginners to established acts. Thursday is Crash Test, when students from the Garage's stand-up classes try out their first jokes. And every month there are themed nights: Trash Comedy Night, Point G on couples and sex, the Apérost roasts, and Les Mécaniciennes, an all-women line-up. Before and after the show, stay at the bar for a cocktail, a pint and tapas to share: charcuterie and cheese boards, bruschetta, toasted sandwiches. Entry is €9, and a hat goes round at the end. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 6.30pm.
