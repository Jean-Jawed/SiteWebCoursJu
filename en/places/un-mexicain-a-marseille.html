Un Mexicain à Marseille

Mexican snack bar with giant portions

A 100% Mexican snack bar on little Place Paul-Cézanne: empanadas, giant quesadillas with melted cheese, burritos, nachos with guacamole and tacos, with halal options. A few seats on the terrace, lots to take away. Come properly hungry.
