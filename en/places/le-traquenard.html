Le Traquenard

The La Plaine bar that crowns Miss Cagole

A popular bar on place Jean-Jaurès, with a red room and a terrace on the square: cheap pints, weekday happy hour, OM matches. Every autumn it hosts the Miss Cagole contest, a celebration of the free, flamboyant and outspoken women of Marseille.
