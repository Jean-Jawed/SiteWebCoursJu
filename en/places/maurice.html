Maurice

Neighbourhood café, bar and restaurant on rue de Lodi

The former Entre Temps bar-tabac on rue de Lodi, now a café, bar and restaurant open from morning to night. A seven-metre counter painted with a mural, vinyl turntables, dice and cards to borrow, La Provence every morning, and a large glass-roofed patio. In the evening, honest seasonal cooking (bluefin tuna, pork belly, Mediterranean oysters, aged beef rib to share) and a fine selection of natural wines.
