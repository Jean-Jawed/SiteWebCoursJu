Atelier 72

Shared artists' studio and gallery on rue Vian

At 8 rue Vian, behind a discreet shopfront, a big New York loft-style studio shared by a small group of artists and designers: photography, illustration, printmaking, sensory mapping, micro-painting and sound art. They work here all year round, and the doors regularly open to the public, free of charge: members' openings, exhibitions such as « Un miroir complice », Sebastian Sarti's drawings about Central America, a printed-art weekend and a Christmas market with prints, posters and screen-printed T-shirts. The studio also runs life-drawing sessions, film-concerts and experimental evenings, and publishes GRAVE, its collective fanzine. A place to see the neighbourhood's art where it is made; the programme is on Instagram.
