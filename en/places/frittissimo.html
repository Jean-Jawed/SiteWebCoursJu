Frittissimo

Italian fried snacks and pinsa romana

An Italian street-food counter under a yellow-and-white awning. "Tapas, yes, but Italian-style": Sicilian panelle, breaded mozzarella balls, courgette flowers, pecorino croquettes, battered sage leaves, arancini, supplì, panzerotti, served in paper trays for a few euros each. Pinsa romana too. A few tables outside, and Italian aperitivo evenings to practise the language.
