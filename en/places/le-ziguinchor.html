Le Ziguinchor

The neighbourhood's Senegalese restaurant, on rue Pastoret

Ziguinchor is the name of the main city of Casamance, in southern Senegal. On rue Pastoret it is an institution: doors painted in Senegal's colours, a yellow-walled dining room with a map of Africa, bigger than it looks from the street, and a little patio for warm evenings. To start, pastels, fatayas and accras; then the great classics, yassa with lemon and onions, mafé in peanut sauce, thieboudienne, the national dish of fish and red rice, with aloco on the side. Drink a home-made ginger or bissap juice, or one of the house flavoured rums, and finish with fanaye or the Douceur de Casamance. Open evenings, Tuesday to Sunday, from 7:30pm.
