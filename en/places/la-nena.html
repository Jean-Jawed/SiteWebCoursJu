La Nena

Bar à tapas des Asturies

An Asturian tapas bar set up in 2023 in a former hair salon on Place Notre-Dame-du-Mont. Cider poured the Asturian way, croquetas, tortilla, pulpo and an award-winning cachopo, to share on the terrace under the plane tree. Tuesday to Saturday, from 6pm.
