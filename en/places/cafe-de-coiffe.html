Café Dé-coiffé

Coffee shop and salon for curly hair

A hybrid spot on rue de la Bibliothèque: on one side a pink coffee shop (matcha, speciality coffee, pastries), on the other a hair salon dedicated to wavy, curly and coily hair. Workshops and creative meet-ups on some weekends.
