Café Zitoune

Specialty coffee and Mediterranean canteen

Half café, half canteen, in a former artist's studio with a red spiral staircase. Specialty coffee, breakfast until 11:30am, then cooking that looks to North Africa and the Middle East: open sandwiches, aïoli, seasonal salads, and the zitoune cookie with black olives. A few tables on the pavement, and a real welcome for children. Tuesday to Saturday, daytime.
