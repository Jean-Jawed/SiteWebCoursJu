Bar Central

Wood-fired pizzas, wine and music

"Pizz, wine, music": a wine and pizza bar on rue Crudère. Pizzas come out of a wood-fired oven with seasonal toppings, to share over a glass of natural wine or a house cocktail (Negroni, Espresso Martini, Paloma). The terrace stretches along the street's painted walls. Tuesday to Saturday, 6pm to 2am; online booking.
