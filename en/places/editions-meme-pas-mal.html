Éditions Même pas mal

Alternative comics publisher, shop and exhibitions

A bright red shopfront on rue des Trois Rois, a big M in a white circle, and painted lettering: comics, fanzines, posters, T-shirts. Même pas mal is an alternative comics publisher, born in Marseille around 2010, which describes itself as free and stubborn. It publishes young authors such as Salomé Lahoche (Ernestine), Maëva Tur (Ariane rêve), Léa Jarrin (Tous les mêmes) and Bathroom Quest (L'Odeur du fer). The premises are both shop and bookshop: the whole catalogue is here, along with fanzines, posters and illustrated objects. At the back, under the vaulted ceiling, a small room hosts exhibitions of original artwork, signings, book-launch parties and small gigs. Open in the afternoon, Tuesday to Friday.
